About

Most business websites fail in the same way. They look fine, but they load slowly, nobody finds them on Google, and the enquiry button does not work properly on a phone.

I fix all three. I build the site, test every form and WhatsApp link, tune the speed, and set up Google Analytics and Search Console so you can see where visitors come from and which pages bring in leads. My SEO work has lifted lead generation by 35% and earned Google Top 3 rankings for several competitive keywords.

I am based in Nairobi and hold a Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Computer Science from the Technical University of Mombasa.

Build it, keep it running, get it found and measure what it brings in.

Web development

Custom sites in PHP, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, plus WordPress, Elementor, Webflow, Shopify and Squarespace. Responsive on every screen and light enough to load quickly.

On-page SEO

Keyword research, title tags, meta descriptions, headings, content structure and internal linking. Local pages and content written for answer engines and AI search (AEO and GEO).

Off-page SEO

Link building, local business listings and citations, and brand mentions that build your site's authority and help it rank for competitive keywords.

Technical SEO

Site speed, crawling and indexing, schema markup, clean site structure and Core Web Vitals. I have taken sites from a PageSpeed score of 45 to 92+.

Website operations

Publishing, content checks, mobile and desktop testing, and fixes followed through to a retest. Hosting, DNS, SSL, backups and security are covered.

Analytics and lead generation

GA4, Search Console and Tag Manager set up properly, with UTM tracking and conversion events. I test forms and WhatsApp links, then report on traffic, enquiries and what to do next.
